About Queen Vee's

Queen Vee's Collections is a premier luxury cosmetics brand founded on the principles of quality, elegance, and technological innovation. We combine ancient beauty secrets with modern AI diagnostics to provide personalized skincare solutions.

Our mission is to empower every individual to feel like royalty through high-performance products that are safe, ethical, and effective.

User Guide & How-To

1. Browsing Products

Click on any product image to see full descriptions, prices, and customer testimonials.

2. Using the AI Advisor

Visit the AI section to upload your photo for a customized beauty routine recommendation.

3. Placing an Order

Add items to your bag. Go to 'Account' to login with your number. Choose Pay on Delivery or M-Pesa. You will hear a sound confirmation once placed!

4. Tracking

Use the 'Order Delivery' menu to see the real-time status of your package on the map.
